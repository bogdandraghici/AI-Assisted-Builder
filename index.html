02 The same screen, with that step open

The same screen, already opened: the plan folds into a spine and the chat holds its place, so settling a step never costs you the argument that produced it. Three decisions on the right — one open, one answered, one handed back. Choosing a row on an orange card is free; Apply is what lands it, and it offers nothing until you have chosen. The last row carries no answer of mine — take Something else and a field opens under it, focused: what you write is the answer, Enter or Apply lands it, and it becomes the settled sentence in your own words. Change on a settled one opens it where it sits and asks the same two acts: the selection starts on the answer already there, so Apply offers nothing until you move it, and leaving without applying keeps the answer you had. A decision stops being mine the moment you apply any row on it, the one I proposed included. Close the step with the ×, Esc, the breadcrumb or the back arrow.

At the foot of the pane is the step's own act. Build this step is withdrawn while the step wears any wash, and the sentence beside it names both kinds separately, because clearing one does not clear the other. Settle the argument and the question and it arms; press it and the step's word becomes Building — it keeps its place under Not built yet, because a step being built is not built. Then click step 4 in the spine and walk on: settling the plan one step at a time is what eventually arms Build the plan.
