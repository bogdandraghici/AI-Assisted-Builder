07 — Space, radius, proportion
{{ p.bar }}
{{ p.v }}
{{ d.box }}
{{ d.v }}
Base 2, and the ramp is used unevenly on purpose: 8, 12 and 16 carry almost all of it. A card's three bands are the only place 10 and 14 appear as padding — 12/16 head, 14/16 reply, 10/16 acts. Nothing sits between two steps — an 11 or a 20 in the source is a mistake, not a nuance.
Radius climbs with the size of the thing it holds — 2 for a tick or dot, 3 for a bar in a wireframe, 4 for a badge, 6 for an icon button or a step chip, 8 for a button, a card or a choice row, 12 for a pane or the shell, 99 for anything round by nature. Nothing between.
Canvas 1440 × 900, shell radius 12. The chat is a fixed 360 in every state but full screen. Everything right of it is one grid: plan and step at 300 · 780, the run at 531 · 321, full screen at 852 · 0 — measured, and they sum to 852 exactly. Prose caps at 800 in a pane and 62ch on a card, so a line stays readable.

09 — The signature gesture, the spine
The plan carries one gesture: a 2px spine down the left with a node per step. The spine is solid where the plan is real and dashed the moment it stops being, so how far the build has actually got is visible without reading a word.
Three nodes, and status is never said twice: a green disc with a tick is built, a dashed ring is not built yet, and a solid ring holding a person is waiting on you. There is no orange node — a step being argued about is still simply not built, and what is contested is said by the card under the row.
node 20 → 8 · spine 2px · gutter 20 → 8 · row gap 16 → 12
A boundary sits between the built group and the rest, under mono headings tinted to the state they name — green for Built, the dim tier for Not built yet. The run draws its own boundary one column further right, because a run can only claim what exists, which is the looser word.
Resources are named in one place only — behind the step's technical disclosure, where the scope is worth having. Nothing else on the screen counts them, and no rail draws them.

One curve carries the product — cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0.2, 1) — at 160ms for a hover and 340ms for the one move. The other two curves exist only to make something lead or lag that move: the measure a title wraps to, and the line box reserved before the text needs it. Rewrapping is not motion, it is a stack of 18px jumps, and no curve smooths a jump. Anything not on this list does not move, and someone who has asked not to be animated at gets the same two states with every duration at zero.
